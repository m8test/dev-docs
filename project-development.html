开发项目

本页面介绍如何一步一步开发一个M8Test脚本项目.

  1. 创建文件 settings.config.yaml 文件, 内容如下

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/kotlin/Basic/settings.config.yaml

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/groovy/Basic/settings.config.yaml

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/javascript/Basic/settings.config.yaml

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/lua/Basic/settings.config.yaml

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/python/Basic/settings.config.yaml

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/ruby/Basic/settings.config.yaml

其中指定了执行settings和init.settings文件脚本语言名称、下载地址以及版本

  1. 创建 settings 文件, 每种语言所需要的扩展名不一样

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/kotlin/Basic//settings.kts

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/groovy/Basic//settings.groovy

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/javascript/Basic//settings.js

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/lua/Basic//settings.lua

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/php/Basic//settings.php

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/python/Basic//settings.py

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/ruby/Basic//settings.rb

这里简单的打印一句话, 如果包含子项目可以在此文件中配置, 一个项目中只需要一个 settings 文件, 子项目中不需要 settings 文件

  1. 创建 build 文件, 每种语言所需要的扩展名不一样

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/kotlin/Basic//build.kts

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/groovy/Basic//build.groovy

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/javascript/Basic//build.js

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/lua/Basic//build.lua

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/php/Basic//build.php

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/python/Basic//build.py

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/ruby/Basic//build.rb

其中配置语言插件以及项目基本信息(脚本入口文件、包名以及logo)

  1. 创建目录 src 用于存放脚本源码, 新建上一步骤配置的入口文件

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/kotlin/Basic/src/com/example/script/primary.kts

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/groovy/Basic/src/com/example/script/primary.groovy

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/python/Basic/src/com/example/script/primary.py

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m8test/SampleCode/refs/heads/main/projects/ruby/Basic/src/com/example/script/primary.rb

  1. 创建 res 目录, 并创建第3步配置的logo文件即可实现最简单的项目
